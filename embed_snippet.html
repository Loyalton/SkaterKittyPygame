TAP TO PLAY
Skater Kitty · loads in a few seconds
1Wait for the loading bar to fill
2Then TAP the screen once to start

On a phone: tap Full screen for bigger buttons. Play upright (buttons under the game) or turn your phone sideways. Leaving the page, locking the phone or tapping Exit stops the music and resets the game.

Keyboard: D = speed up, A = slow down, Space = ollie (hold, then let go), Shift = grind.
Touch: left pad = BRAKE (top) / PUSH (bottom), right pad = GRIND (top) / JUMP (bottom). Keep your thumb down and slide between the zones.
